# Human Design Dictionary

Compact vocabulary for the Human Design Atlas. Use this as a lookup interface: each entry gives the shared meaning in one or two sentences, while deeper notes can expand the concept elsewhere.

## System vocabulary

- **Type:** One of five Human Design energy-pattern categories: Generator, Manifesting Generator, Projector, Manifestor, or Reflector. It describes how a chart is said to engage with life, not a scientifically established personality category.
- **Strategy:** The Type-specific way Human Design proposes that a person engage with life: responding, responding then informing, waiting for invitation, informing, or waiting through a lunar cycle.
- **Authority:** The chart mechanism Human Design uses to describe decision-making. It is a symbolic system concept, not an empirically validated decision procedure.
- **Signature:** The experience traditionally associated with moving in alignment with one’s Type and Strategy.
- **Not-Self Theme:** The experience traditionally associated with conditioning, resistance, or moving away from one’s Type Strategy. It is a contemplative prompt, not a diagnosis.
- **Design:** The chart layer calculated from a prenatal point approximately 88 solar degrees before birth, traditionally associated with unconscious or bodily patterns.
- **Personality:** The chart layer calculated from the birth moment, traditionally associated with conscious or more readily recognized patterns.
- **Activation:** The placement of a chart point in a Gate and Line, such as `45.5` for Gate 45, Line 5.

## BodyGraph vocabulary

- **Gate:** One of 64 archetypal positions mapped from the I Ching into the Human Design BodyGraph.
- **Line:** One of six modes of expression associated with a Gate activation.
- **Center:** One of nine symbolic energy and awareness centers in the BodyGraph.
- **Channel:** A connection between two Gates that links two Centers and is interpreted as a more consistent energetic theme.
- **Definition:** The pattern of connected Centers in a BodyGraph, including Single, Split, Triple Split, and Quadruple Split Definition.
- **Profile:** A two-number combination derived from the six I Ching lines, traditionally used to describe a person’s role or learning pattern.
- **Incarnation Cross:** A life-theme synthesis based primarily on the Personality and Design Sun and Earth activations.

## Source and evidence vocabulary

- **System Description:** An explanation of how a Human Design school or source defines a concept internally.
- **Practitioner Interpretation:** An interpretation offered by a teacher, school, or practitioner; it should not automatically be treated as universal to all Human Design traditions.
- **Contemplative Prompt:** A question or observation exercise offered for self-reflection rather than proof of a fixed trait.
- **Empirical Claim:** A claim about the physical, psychological, or social world that requires evidence outside the Human Design system.
- **Source Scope:** The tradition or evidence category a note belongs to, such as `human-design-system`, `gene-keys`, `practitioner`, `scientific-commentary`, or `vault-synthesis`.
- **Validation Status:** The current review state of a note: `draft`, `reviewed`, `stable`, `source-disagreement`, or `not-yet-researched`.

## How to expand an entry

Keep the dictionary definition compact. When a concept needs mechanics, examples, history, or competing interpretations, create a dedicated note and link to it from the relevant entry rather than turning the dictionary into an encyclopedia.
