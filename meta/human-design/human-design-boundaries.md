# Human Design Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines what the Human Design Atlas owns, what it connects to, and what it deliberately leaves outside its scope.

The boundary protects the vault from becoming an accidental encyclopedia of astrology, the I Ching, spirituality, psychology, genetics, or medicine. Related domains may be linked through compact interface notes, but they retain their own intellectual ownership.

## Repository identity

The Human Design Atlas documents Human Design as an esoteric and contemplative knowledge system. It describes the system’s internal mechanics, vocabulary, interpretive traditions, and practical reflection exercises.

It does not establish Human Design as scientifically validated psychology, genetics, medicine, or destiny.

## What this repository owns

The vault may develop substantial content for:

- Human Design’s BodyGraph and chart structure;
- Design and Personality;
- planetary activations as used by Human Design;
- Gates, Lines, Channels, and Centers;
- the five Types;
- Strategy, Authority, Signature, and Not-Self Theme;
- Definition, Profiles, and Incarnation Crosses;
- Human Design reading methods;
- contemplative questions and self-observation practices;
- source-aware comparisons between Human Design schools;
- consented or anonymized case studies;
- the relationships among these Human Design concepts.

The interface rule is simple:

> Define only enough of an adjacent domain to explain the Human Design connection, then link outward rather than recreating the whole field.

## Explicitly out of scope

The repository does not aim to become:

- a complete I Ching reference work;
- a general astrology vault;
- a medical or genetic advice system;
- a psychological diagnostic tool;
- a substitute for therapy, medical care, or professional expertise;
- a universal authority on contested Human Design interpretations;
- a repository of private charts without consent;
- a collection of unattributed claims presented as fact.

## Source-scope labels

Every substantial note or claim should make its scope legible.

- `human-design-system` — describes Human Design’s internal mechanics or terminology.
- `gene-keys` — describes Richard Rudd’s related contemplative system.
- `practitioner` — reflects a teacher, school, or practitioner interpretation.
- `historical-comparative` — explains an adjacent tradition or historical relationship.
- `scientific-commentary` — discusses evidence or critique from outside the system.
- `vault-synthesis` — organizes or explains material in the voice of this repository.

These labels describe provenance and function. They do not imply that every source has equal authority or evidentiary status.

## Claim classes

Use the following distinction when writing:

- **System description:** “Human Design defines…”
- **Practitioner interpretation:** “Some practitioners interpret…”
- **Contemplative prompt:** “A person might explore…”
- **Empirical claim:** “Research would be needed to establish…”
- **Boundary statement:** “This repository does not treat…”

Avoid turning a system description into an empirical claim through confident wording.

## Boundary decision test

Before adding a page, ask:

1. Is this primarily about Human Design’s own vocabulary or mechanics?
2. Does it help explain a relationship inside the Human Design model?
3. Can the note identify its source scope?
4. Would a short interface definition and an external link be enough?
5. Does it introduce health, psychological, genetic, or destiny claims that need stronger evidence?
6. Is the content about a person’s chart, and if so, is consent clear?

If the topic is adjacent rather than central, prefer a compact interface entry in the dictionary or a source note.

## Boundary maintenance

When a new domain repeatedly appears in the vault:

1. Add a one- or two-sentence interface definition to the dictionary.
2. Record the domain and its source scope in the source index.
3. Link to a stronger external or future dedicated knowledge source.
4. Only create a full local branch if it materially improves Human Design understanding.

This keeps the repository open and connected without allowing its center of gravity to drift.
