# Strategy

## Overview

Strategy is the Type-specific way Human Design proposes that a person engage with life. It is intended to be experimented with rather than followed as a rigid command.

## Core mechanics

| Type | Strategy |
| --- | --- |
| Generator | Wait to respond |
| Manifesting Generator | Respond, then inform |
| Projector | Wait for recognition and invitation |
| Manifestor | Inform before acting |
| Reflector | Wait through a lunar cycle for major decisions |

## Relationships

Strategy is paired with [Type](type.md) and interpreted alongside [Authority](authority.md). The traditional result of alignment is called the [Signature](signature.md); the friction pattern is called the [Not-Self Theme](not-self-theme.md).

## Contemplative interpretation

Strategy can be treated as an experiment in timing: notice what changes when action follows the proposed sequence rather than mental urgency.

## Boundaries and uncertainty

Strategy belongs to Human Design’s internal system mechanics. It should not override practical responsibility, consent, communication, or professional advice.
