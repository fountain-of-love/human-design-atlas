# Signature

## Overview

Signature is the experience Human Design traditionally associates with moving in alignment with one’s Type and Strategy.

## Core mechanics

| Type | Traditional Signature |
| --- | --- |
| Generator | Satisfaction |
| Manifesting Generator | Satisfaction |
| Projector | Success |
| Manifestor | Peace |
| Reflector | Surprise |

## Relationships

Signature is paired with [Not-Self Theme](not-self-theme.md), [Type](type.md), and [Strategy](strategy.md).

## Contemplative interpretation

Signature is best used as an observation prompt: “What quality appears when my energy is engaged in a way that feels correct?”

## Boundaries and uncertainty

Signature is a subjective interpretive category. It is not a clinical measure of wellbeing or proof that a decision was objectively correct.
