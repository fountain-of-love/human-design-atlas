# Design and Personality

## Overview

Human Design charts contain two calculation layers: Personality and Design. Together they produce the activations used to construct the BodyGraph.

## Core mechanics

- **Personality:** calculated from the birth moment and traditionally associated with conscious or recognizable patterns.
- **Design:** calculated from a prenatal point approximately 88 solar degrees before birth and traditionally associated with unconscious or bodily patterns.

Each layer contains planetary activations expressed as Gates and Lines.

## Relationships

See [Activation](activation.md), [Gate](../../meta/human-design/human-design-dictionary.md#gate), [Line](../../meta/human-design/human-design-dictionary.md#line), and [The 13 Elements](../HUMAN-DESIGN-13-ELEMENTS.md).

## Contemplative interpretation

The two layers can be used as a reflection on the difference between what a person consciously identifies with and what may appear through body, habit, or repeated experience.

## Boundaries and uncertainty

The Design/Personality distinction is part of Human Design’s esoteric calculation model. It should not be treated as a scientifically established division between conscious and unconscious biology.
