# Authority

## Overview

Authority is Human Design’s proposed decision-making mechanism. It directs attention away from making every decision through mental analysis and toward the chart’s defined awareness or energy centers.

## Core mechanics

Common forms include Emotional, Sacral, Splenic, Ego, Self-Projected, Mental, and Lunar Authority. The exact Authority depends on the chart’s definition and center hierarchy.

## Relationships

Authority is read after [Type](type.md) and [Strategy](strategy.md). It is also related to the Solar Plexus, Sacral Center, and Spleen, which are listed in the [dictionary](../../meta/human-design/human-design-dictionary.md#center) and planned as future Center notes.

## Contemplative interpretation

Authority can be approached as a prompt to notice where clarity appears in lived experience: emotional timing, bodily response, instinct, voice, will, or environmental sounding-board processes.

## Boundaries and uncertainty

Authority is a symbolic system model, not an empirically validated decision procedure. It should not replace safety checks, expertise, or informed deliberation.
