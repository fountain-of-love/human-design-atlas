# Activation

## Overview

An activation is a planetary or chart-point placement in a Human Design Gate and Line. It is commonly written as `Gate.Line`, such as `45.5`.

## Core mechanics

- The number before the decimal identifies one of the 64 Gates.
- The number after the decimal identifies one of six Lines.
- Activations occur on both the Design and Personality sides of the chart.
- Connected activations can contribute to Channels and Centers.

## Relationships

Activation connects [Design and Personality](design-and-personality.md), [Gate](../../meta/human-design/human-design-dictionary.md#gate), [Line](../../meta/human-design/human-design-dictionary.md#line), [Channel](../../meta/human-design/human-design-dictionary.md#channel), and the [13 elements](../HUMAN-DESIGN-13-ELEMENTS.md).

## Contemplative interpretation

An activation can be treated as a symbolic lens: the planetary role frames the theme, the Gate supplies the archetypal subject, and the Line suggests a style of expression.

## Boundaries and uncertainty

Activation meanings vary across schools and practitioners. A single activation should not be used to infer a complete personality or life outcome.
