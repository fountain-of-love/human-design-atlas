# Human Design Worked Example: Yves’s Chart

This document is a separate case study for the general model described in [The 13 Elements of a Human Design Chart](../HUMAN-DESIGN-13-ELEMENTS.md).

Yves’s chart is used as a concrete example because it is the chart of the document owner. The purpose is to demonstrate how planetary activations can be read in practice—not to make one person’s chart the definition of Human Design, or to present a complete personal reading.

> Human Design is an esoteric and contemplative system. The interpretations below are symbolic prompts, not scientifically validated descriptions of personality, health, or destiny.

## 2. The reading method

This chart demonstrates three basic reading steps:

1. Read each planetary point as a lens.
2. Read the gate and line as the specific activation.
3. Place the activation back into the complete BodyGraph before drawing conclusions.

For example, `45.5` means Gate 45, Line 5. The planetary role adds one interpretive layer; the gate adds the archetypal theme; the line adds a style of expression.

## 3. Repeated gates

Some gates appear on both sides, though often with different line numbers:

- **Gate 39** appears through Design Mercury and Design Mars.
- **Gate 26** appears through Design Earth and Design Saturn.
- **Gate 11** appears through Design Uranus and Personality Uranus.
- **Gate 58** appears through Design Neptune and Personality Neptune.

Within Human Design, repetition can be treated as an invitation to pay attention. It may suggest a theme emphasized from both conscious and unconscious perspectives. The same method can be applied to any chart; the point is not that repetition proves a trait.

## 4. Sample thematic observations

### Gate 11 and Gate 58

The example shows Gate 11 through Uranus on both sides and Gate 58 through Neptune on both sides. As a contemplative prompt, this combination could invite reflection on unusual ideas, freshness, vitality, and the desire to improve or enliven life.

This is not a complete interpretation. A fuller reading would consider the gates’ centers, channels, line meanings, and relationship to Type, Authority, and Profile.

### Gate 45 and Gate 26

The Design Sun is shown in Gate 45, while Design Earth and Design Saturn are shown in Gate 26. Since Sun and Earth are foundational activations, this pairing could be explored as a relationship between leadership, stewardship, resources, influence, and communicating value responsibly.

This is a reflective theme, not a prediction or a claim about fixed character.

## 5. Chart context

The summary beneath the BodyGraph identifies:

- **Type:** Projector
- **Strategy:** Wait for the Invitation
- **Inner Authority:** Splenic
- **Definition:** Triple Split Definition
- **Profile:** 3/5
- **Incarnation Cross:** Right Angle Cross of Rulership, listed as 47/22 | 45/26

This context matters because a planetary activation does not stand alone. In the system’s own logic, Authority remains the primary decision-making mechanism, while Type, Strategy, Profile, channels, centers, and definition shape how the themes are lived.

## 6. What this case study is—and is not

This case study is:

- an example of how to organize real chart data;
- a demonstration of the distinction between Design and Personality;
- an illustration of how repeated activations may be noticed;
- a prompt for observing whether symbolic themes resonate with lived experience.

It is not:

- a complete personal reading;
- a universal template for Projectors or 3/5 Profiles;
- a diagnosis or psychological assessment;
- a substitute for practical, medical, legal, or mental-health guidance.
